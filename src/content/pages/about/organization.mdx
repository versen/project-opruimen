## How we are organized

* VERSEN is an informal and flat organization with a relatively large [board](/about/board/) and a large group of [members](/about/members/). Members are from different institutes, universities, companies, and other organizations, who share a common responsibility and enthusiasm for the field of software engineering and the drive to improve it.
* CWI (Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica) is the administrative home of VERSEN.
* VERSEN organizes the yearly [SEN Symposium](https://www.sen-symposium.nl/).
* VERSEN meets regularly to evaluate past actions and make plans for the future.
* [Working groups](/working-groups/) are our way of effectively working together towards a specific goal.
