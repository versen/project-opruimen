The **Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda (SRIA) for the Future of Software** sets out why software is the foundation of Europe's digital transformation, and which research and innovation it needs in the coming years. It was published in September 2026 by Informatics Europe and ERCIM. Alexander Serebrenik, Tijs van der Storm and Tanja Vos of VERSEN are part of its writing team, and more VERSEN members contributed.
