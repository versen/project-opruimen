Writing a competitive grant proposal is challenging, and studying funded proposals can help a great deal. Within VERSEN, we have often discussed sharing successful applications to help other software researchers, and this page is the result.

The researchers below have received a grant from NWO or the ERC on a topic within the area of interest of VERSEN, and have agreed to be listed here. If you are preparing a proposal for one of these schemes and think it would help to see one of these applications, feel free to contact the researcher directly. Please keep in mind that sharing is voluntary: it is up to each researcher to decide whether to share their proposal.

Have you received a grant in one of these (or other) schemes, and would you like to be added to this list? Or did we list something incorrectly? Please contact us at [info@versen.nl](mailto:info@versen.nl). This overview is updated periodically.

*Last updated: September 2026.*
