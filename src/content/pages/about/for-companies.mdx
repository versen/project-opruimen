## Host an MSc thesis student

VERSEN connects companies with university research groups for MSc thesis projects on software science and engineering. Read how the [MSc thesis collaborations](/about/msc-thesis-collaborations/) work and how to propose a project.

## Advise us

Our [advisory board](/about/advisory-board/) brings the view of industry into VERSEN.

## Support VERSEN

Membership is free for individuals because institutions sponsor the association. See our [sponsors and partners](/about/partners-and-sponsors/), or write to the treasurer through the [contact page](/contact/) to discuss a contribution.

## Reach the research community

Looking for expertise on a software engineering topic, or want to announce something to researchers? Use the [contact page](/contact/), or [suggest a news item](/suggest-news/) for the website and newsletter.
