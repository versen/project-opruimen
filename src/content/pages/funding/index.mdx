## Funding for your initiative

VERSEN has a yearly budget for actions that support its mission. Next to the board, any member may request funding for a project that is in line with this mission, most often an event.

1. Read the [Requests for funding HOWTO](/assets/pdf/funding-v1.1.pdf), which describes what can be funded and what a request should contain.
2. Send your request to [funding@versen.nl](mailto:funding@versen.nl).
3. The [Funding working group](/working-groups/funding/) reviews it and advises the [Executive Board](/working-groups/executive-board/), and you hear the decision and the feedback from the group.

## Help with a grant proposal

Writing a proposal for NWO or the ERC? On the [Proposal support](/resources/proposal-support/) page, members who were awarded a Veni, Vidi, Vici, ENW or ERC grant offer to share their funded proposal with you.

The slides and material of our 2025 afternoon [How to Navigate the SE Funding Landscape](/events/2025-navigate-funding/) are also available.
