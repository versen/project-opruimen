The goal of the executive board is to manage, animate and improve the entire VERSEN community. They also have formal responsibilities as 
listed in the VERSEN charter, one of which is to write the [annual reports](/resources/annual-reports/).

The current members and their roles, the full board and the history of the executive board are on the [board page](/about/board/). The executive board meets monthly, and any member of the VERSEN community is welcome to join.
