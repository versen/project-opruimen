VERSEN is pleased to announce the call for submissions for the 2025 VERSEN Thesis Awards. Nominations for both the MSc Thesis Award and the PhD Thesis Award are invited.

**Important dates**

- 28 January 2026 — Submission deadline
- 25 February 2026 — Notification of results

The joint MSc and PhD award ceremony will take place at the SEN Symposium 2026.

**Eligibility**

The thesis must have been completed at a Dutch institute of higher academic learning, with the degree obtained between 1 September 2024 and 31 August 2025.

All submissions must be made through the official submission form: [https://www.versen.nl/contents/awards-cfs](https://www.versen.nl/contents/awards-cfs)

**Further information**

For questions about the awards or the submission process, please contact dr. Georgiana Caltais — [g.g.c.caltais@utwente.nl](mailto:g.g.c.caltais@utwente.nl).
