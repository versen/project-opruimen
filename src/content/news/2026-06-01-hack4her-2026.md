---
title: "Women in Tech Initiative Expands Nationally with Hack4Her 2026"
date: 2026-06-01
---

In the latest edition of I/O Magazine, Katja Tuma shares her vision for creating a more inclusive and supportive environment for women in technology.

What began as a local initiative is now expanding nationally: Hack4Her 2026 will take place in both Amsterdam and Eindhoven, bringing together students, researchers, and technology enthusiasts from across the Netherlands. The initiative addresses a persistent challenge in computer science: not only attracting more women to the field, but also ensuring they remain engaged and empowered throughout their studies and careers.

Read the full article, "Empowering Women in Tech", in [I/O Magazine #1 2026](https://ict-research.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/IO-magazine-NR1-2026-online.pdf).
